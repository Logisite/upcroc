Cadence

Everything here is a bucket filling and draining on a fixed cadence. The product has no personality at all — it has an interval. This style treats that as the whole idea and refuses to pretend otherwise.

No animal, no eye, no heat. Just a measure of elapsed time, marked, and one tick that says now. It is the least charming of the four and it is the one that looks the most expensive.

Philosophy

Watch tools are usually illustrated with charts, waveforms, or a magnifying glass — all promises about information. Cadence shows a count instead: four marks for four checks, and a fifth that is longer than the others because it is the one that is about to matter. Legibility is the aesthetic here.

The four devices. 1. Letterform fused with symbol — the outer form is the c again, but this time it is a cadence arc: a measured 270° of sweep, like a dial that has been running. 2. Gradient on one element only — the arc runs ember→amber so the direction of time is legible in the colour itself. 3. The ticks are a covelet set: they cut inward from the arc, so the negative space between them does the measuring. 4. Exactly one tick is longer and pale. The asymmetry is the whole mark.

Story

The arc270° of a circle, open at the upper right. It is the letter c, and it is also a dial that has already done most of a round.
The ticksFour marks inside the arc at even intervals. They are the cadence — the app checking, and checking again.
The handOne long pale tick, past the fourth, angled outward. It is "now", and it is the only element allowed to be the lightest colour.
The groundBark brown, not black. Warm grounds make a measured mark feel considered rather than clinical.

Usage rules

Minimum sizeC1/C2 at 64px — this is the one that cannot be small. Below 64 use C4.
The handAlways the lightest thing in the mark. If the hand is not the brightest, the mark is wrong.
WordmarkSerif. The sans version of this identity reads as a different company.
MotionThe hand advances one tick per completed check. Nothing else animates, ever.
