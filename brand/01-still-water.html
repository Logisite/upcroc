Still Water

A crocodile does not chase. It lies flat for hours, absolutely still, then moves once. That is the product: a long quiet wait and one brief decisive moment.

The mark fuses a letterform with a symbol, which is what the strongest identity work of this kind does. The lens is not closed — it breaks on the right, so it is simultaneously an eye and a c. You read the name and you see the eye, and neither meaning has to be explained.

Philosophy

Monitoring tools are built to look busy so you never feel in control. upcroc should look calm on your home screen and urgent for the two seconds that matter. The gold is rationed: it appears only where something is caught.

The four devices doing the work. 1. Letterform fused with symbol — an open lens is an eye and a c at the same time, so the wordmark and the mark are one object. 2. Gradient on one element only — the lens runs gold to ember, the pupil stays flat pale. 3. The pupil stands free inside the lens instead of being cut out of it, which is the crafted-linework trick: it reads at any size and works on a transparent ground. 4. The lens stops short of closing. A closed eye reads as finished; an open one reads as still waiting.

Story

The lensOne continuous stroke with round caps, drawn as a lens and left open on the right. That opening is what makes it a c, and what makes it an eye that is still awake.
The pupilA flat pale capsule standing alone in the middle. Vertical, because reptilian pupils are. It never moves and never animates.
GradientGold into ember, on the lens only. Warm where the light lands, cooling as the stroke runs out of breath.
The gapDeliberate. The app spends its life unfinished and waiting, so the mark is unfinished on purpose.

Usage rules

Minimum sizeA1/A2 at 48px; below that A4, which drops the gradient for legibility.
GradientOn the lens only. Never on the pupil, never on the wordmark's "up".
The gapDo not close it. The openness is what makes it an eye and a c, and it is the meaning.
MotionNone. If it ever moves, it is because a job landed.
