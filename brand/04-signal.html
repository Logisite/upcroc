Brand style 04 of 04

Signal

The quietest identity of the four, and the one with the most attitude. Ink on paper, one hot colour, nothing decorative. It comes from editorial and instrument design rather than from app branding at all.

Four bars. Three are the buckets. The tallest is not a bar — it is the pupil, the same vertical slit that carries styles 1, so the set reads as one family rather than four unrelated ideas.

Philosophy

Premium does not have to mean dark mode and gradients. It very often means paper, ink, and exactly one loud colour used once. The hot orange appears on the pupil, on the wordmark's croc, and nowhere else in the entire identity. Rationing one colour that hard is what makes it look expensive.

The four devices. 1. Letterform fused with symbol — the bars are a queue: short, medium, tall, all left-aligned on one baseline, so they are data before they are decoration. 2. Flat, no gradient, anywhere. This is the style that proves the gradient was never load-bearing. 3. The tallest bar is the slit pupil, inherited from style 1 — the recurring motif that ties the set together. 4. The bars are unevenly spaced. Even spacing is a spreadsheet; uneven spacing is a mark.

Story

The barsThree ascending bars on a shared baseline — new, seen, notified. They read as a histogram of the last three checks.
The pupilThe fourth and tallest bar, narrow, in hot orange. It is the one thing that caught. It is the only element in the identity that is allowed to be alarming.
The baselineEverything sits on one line. No bar floats, no shadow, no container. That restraint is the entire look.
The groundPaper. A light icon, which almost nobody does and which instantly separates you from the dark-gradient field.

Usage rules

Minimum sizeD1/D2 at 48px; below that D4, which drops a bar and widens the pupil.
Hot orangeOnce per surface. If you can say it twice, you have already broken it.
GradientNone. This style has none. That is the point of it.
MotionOne bar grows per completed check. The pupil never moves.
