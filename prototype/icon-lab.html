Jaw monogram The U is the jaw, the fangs are cut from the same shape, and the blue ping sits outside the mouth. Fixes the smiley: no eyebrows, no nose.
Safest
Croc head A side-profile snout with an eye bump and a radar sweep behind it. No letter at all. Most distinctive, but risks looking like a games mascot.
Boldest
Radar eye One croc eye, slit pupil, and a sweep ring. Quiet and expensive-looking. Strong at 48 px, but can read as a vision or security app.
Premium
Pure sweep No animal and no letter. A radar dial with one blue quarter and a blip. Cleanest and most generic; weakest link to the name.
Most neutral
v1 — rejected Blue eyebrows + a blue nose + a hidden ring. Reads as a face, not a croc or a radar.
Do not use
My pick: Jaw monogram. It keeps the name, survives 24 px, and fixes the accidental face. Radar eye is the more premium art direction if you want zero letterforms. Tell me which one and I'll build the full icon set (splash, home tile, adaptive masks, favicon) around it.
