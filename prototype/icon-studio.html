Croc, side profile

Long snout, open jaw, four fangs, slit eye. The silhouette does the work; the blue is only the eye. No letterform, no radar cliché, no accidental face.

On blackDefault launcher background.
On accentBlue tile, white mark. Use for splash and store art.
MonochromePure white. Notification icon and themed icons.
  1. Head mass — a 192 px circle at (176, 268), with an eye ridge.
  2. Snout — a tapered wedge: 74 px deep at the head, 56 px at the tip.
  3. Jaw — 200 px long, hinged at the head, tapering to the right.
  4. Mouth — one 34 px gap; teeth are triangles inside it.
  5. Eye — 21 px blue iris with a 5.5 × 14.5 slit, set on the ridge.
